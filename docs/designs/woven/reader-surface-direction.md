# Reader surface

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** This surface is a reading room, not a chat transcript with a portrait sidebar. Prose owns the first viewport; the cast and controls recede to a working edge. It refuses the category-default card feed, avatar rows, and permanent dashboard chrome.

**OWN-WORLD.** Deep petrol shell, saffron selection, pale cool reading ground, dark ink prose. Source Serif 4 carries the fiction; Source Sans 3 carries labels and controls. A paired petrol/saffron hairline with a two-diamond interlace marks each role change, and the same split rule returns quietly in empty states.

**STORY.** The visitor understands what is established, what they just contributed, and whether the writer has handled it. They can read for a long stretch, answer in their own voice, or move to Director discussion without confusing either channel.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** A full-width petrol topbar holds the wordmark, Story/Director tabs, and a narrow-screen Cast toggle. Below, a left-aligned reading heading and rule lead into bordered prose passages; the composer sits at the base of the reading column. The disclosed cast is a separate framed column on wide screens and a focused drawer on narrow screens.

**FORM.** Woven anthology, first on the ordered candidate list, selected from the approved direction round. `seed: 2025246f7f020fa4`. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/woven.png`.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Adaptation boundary

The approved comp's scene prose, portraits, and biographies are illustrative. No supplied cards or scene were available. The reader therefore ships empty public logs/cast and renders only writer-disclosed data; synthetic test content is labelled and never saved into the real workspace. The literal comp-spec/plates/hero pixel gates remain open at `spec` in `.impeccable/build/state.json` because reproducing those illustrative assets would violate the approved data boundary. Functional browser evidence, the Woven topology, and the signature role-rule are implemented and tested.
